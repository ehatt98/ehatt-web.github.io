Science Communication

Astrophysics gives us a deep insight into how the universe works in all its beauty and complexity. This has made studying astrophysics personally fulfilling for me, but it can also be a very useful pursuit beyond this. Astrophysicists rely on a good understanding of statistics and computational tools. Therefore outreach is important in two ways; 1. It allows us to give non-astrophysicists a detailed insight into the universe, and 2. it enables us to teach skills that are useful for a wide range of applications outside of space science.

Statistics -- Why you should care?

The second part of my job involves the application of statistical methods, which at times involves the use of machine learning. Statistics is the language through which we can understand data and what it means. A working understanding of stats doesn't just matter for scientists, among other things it can: help you see the truth behind flashy headlines, make better choices with your money and understand the world around you.
